    How to play

    • Choose a move, then apply it to both sides of the equation.
    • Use the highlight button to distinguish variable terms from constants. Sound cues are on by default and can be turned off.
    • Type the new line after simplifying.
    • Use a hint if you get stuck. It will remind you what to do on each side.
    • You may enter a line up to 2 steps ahead. The game accepts it and records the omitted intermediate steps as skipped.
    • Each level mixes several question types. A mistake adds one extra question of that type (once per type).
    • 10 points for a step solved without a hint or retry; 5 points after a hint or retry.
